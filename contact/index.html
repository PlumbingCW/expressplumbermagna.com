How to Reach Us

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call (385) 488-1892. The line is answered around the clock, every day of the year, including holidays. Describe the problem, give your address and cross streets, and tell dispatch whether water is still running.

For leaks, backups, no hot water and anything involving gas, call instead of writing. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

What to Have Ready When You Call

A few details speed everything up. Know where your main water shutoff is, whether the water heater is gas or electric, and roughly how old the home is. If a drain is involved, note which fixtures are affected, because that tells the plumber whether the blockage is in a branch line or the main sewer.

If you can safely take photos of the leak or damage, keep them for your insurance claim. Do not wait for the plumber to move furniture or rugs out of the wet area if you can do it without touching anything electrical.
