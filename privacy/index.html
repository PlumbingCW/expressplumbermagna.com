Privacy Policy

This site takes calls, not form submissions. This page explains what information we handle when you call or browse.

Calls to Our Line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call (385) 488-1892, we may collect your phone number, your address, and the details of your plumbing problem so a plumber can be scheduled and the job can be completed. We use that information to provide the service you asked for and to improve how calls are handled.

This Website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working. We may use basic analytics to see which pages are visited, and we do not use that data to identify individual visitors.

Sharing Information

We share job details with the licensed plumber and dispatch staff who handle your request. We may share information when the law requires it. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing.

Your Choices

You can ask us not to keep your call details by emailing contact@expressplumbermagna.com. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, and the site will keep working. We may update this policy, and the current version is always on this page.
