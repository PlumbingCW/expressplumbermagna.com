An overflowing toilet is an emergency. Close the small valve on the wall behind it by turning it clockwise, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop the flow. Then call the 24/7 line. We handle toilet emergencies at night and on weekends, and a plumber can usually be scheduled the same day.

If other fixtures are gurgling at the same time, the blockage may be in the main line rather than the toilet.

Emergency Toilet Problems: What to Do First

Overflow, a toilet that will not stop running and a leak around the base all damage floors. Shut the supply valve, mop standing water, and put towels around the base. If the toilet is stopped up and rising, do not flush again. Plunging with a flat-flange plunger helps with a simple clog, and a bucket helps remove water from a full bowl.

A leak at the base is the one to act on quickly. The wax ring seals the toilet to the drain, and when it fails, water seeps into the subfloor and rot starts. The toilet is lifted, the old wax is scraped, the flange is checked, and a new seal is set. If the flange is cracked, a repair ring restores a flat, firm surface.

Replacing a Toilet Instead of Repairing It

Replace when the porcelain is cracked, when repairs have piled up, or when an older high-flow fixture wastes water. Modern toilets use far less water per flush, and the savings add up on a bill that already reflects moderately hard Magna water. Choose a model with a rough-in distance that matches your drain, which is measured from the wall to the bolt center.

What a toilet installation includes

  • Shutting off water and removing the old fixture.
  • Inspecting and repairing the flange.
  • Setting a new wax or foam seal and tightening bolts evenly.
  • Connecting the supply line and testing the flush.
  • Checking for leaks at the base and the supply connection.

Hard Water and Fixture Wear

Mineral buildup from Magna's moderately hard water, averaging 105 mg/L in the district's 2023 report, can clog rim jets and sticks fill valve parts. Descaling the jets and replacing a stiff fill valve restores performance without a full replacement.

A toilet that clogs repeatedly may point to a drain problem beyond the bowl. See drain cleaning for that side. A leaking shutoff valve behind the toilet is covered on the faucet and sink repair page.

Code Details Behind Toilet Installation

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with statewide amendments, and your city or county building department enforces it. Toilet installation involves the flange height, the wax or foam seal, the clearance around the bowl and the water supply connection. Replacing a fixture like for like seldom needs a permit, while moving a toilet to a new spot usually does.
