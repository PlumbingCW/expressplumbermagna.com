Where Magna Express Plumbing Works

Dispatch starts at the office on Magna Main Street and works outward. Magna is the home base, and the four towns below are the ones we reach most often. Each town has a page with its own water utility, housing notes and ZIP codes, because a 1960s brick ranch in Kearns does not leak the same way as a newer West Jordan subdivision.

Distances are estimates and traffic changes them. West Valley City and Kearns are the closest neighbors, reached along 3500 South and the SR-201 corridor. Taylorsville and West Jordan are farther out, so same-day windows there depend on where trucks are working.

Calls from Glendale on the Salt Lake City side, and from Stansbury Park and Tooele, are taken case by case. Call the 24/7 line with your address and dispatch will tell you honestly whether a technician can reach you the same day. For Magna itself, see the drain cleaning and leak detection pages.
