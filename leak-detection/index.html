A hidden leak shows up as a symptom long before you see water. The meter keeps moving after every fixture is off, the water bill jumps, a floor feels warm or a ceiling stain spreads. A leak inside a wall or under a slab is an emergency when it is actively wetting framing or electrical, and our 24/7 line can send a plumber at any hour.

Close the main valve if you can see water coming through a ceiling or wall, then check whether the meter stops. If it stops, the leak is inside the house.

Start With the Meter: A Five-Minute Test

Turn everything off

Close all taps and stop the washer, dishwasher and ice maker.

Read the meter

Note the leak indicator or the last digits on the dial.

Wait 15 minutes

Do not use any water during that time.

Read again

Any movement means water is leaking somewhere on your side.

If the meter moves with the house shut off, the next question is where. A leak between the street and the house sits on your service line, and a leak after the shutoff sits inside the structure. That split tells the plumber which tools to bring.

No single tool is perfect, so the plumber combines two or three to confirm the spot before anyone cuts a hole. That keeps the opening in your slab, wall or yard small and makes repair simpler.

Water Meter and High Water Bill Leaks

A bill that doubles without a reason is the most common first sign of a hidden leak. Toilets that run, irrigation lines that crack and water heater relief valves that weep can all drain water unnoticed. Compare your bill to the same month last year, then do the meter test above.

The Magna Water District office, open Monday through Thursday, can confirm your meter read dates when you call. Bring that date and the readings to the plumber, since they narrow down when the leak started.

Ceilings, Walls, Crawlspaces and Yard Lines

A ceiling or wall stain usually traces back to a supply line, a drain joint or a shower pan above. Under-house and crawlspace leaks hide in dark, damp spaces where mold grows quietly. Buried yard lines, including irrigation, leak into soil, often with a soft or green patch on the surface.

A short paragraph is not much on pool and spa leaks, but they are part of the same work. If you have a pool, mention it on the call so the plumber brings the right gear.

The 2020 earthquake and pipe stress

Magna was the center of the March 18, 2020 earthquake sequence, and the state earthquake site reports the most severe damage in the valley was in Magna. If your house developed a new damp spot or a rising bill after that event, a pressure test is a reasonable way to rule out a stressed joint. We can run it without opening walls.

Why We Name the Methods

Leak detection works from physical principles that you can look up, such as sound from pressurized water, temperature differences and pressure decay in an isolated line. Opening walls or slabs afterward is regulated work. Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with statewide amendments, and your city or county building department enforces it. Ask the plumber which method found the leak so you can judge the result.
