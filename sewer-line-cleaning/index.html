A clogged main sewer line stops every drain in the house at once. You may see water backing up in a tub, a toilet that will not flush, or a floor drain that fills in the basement. Treat it as urgent and call the 24/7 line. We take main line calls at night and on weekends, and a plumber can often be scheduled the same day.

Stop using water the moment you suspect the main. Every flush or load of laundry pushes more into the house.

Main Line Emergency: What to Do While You Wait

First steps

  • Stop using all fixtures, including laundry and dishwashers.
  • Find the cleanout, usually a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement.
  • Keep children and pets out of any sewage-affected room.
  • Photograph damage for insurance before cleanup.
  • Open windows for ventilation if sewer gas is noticeable.

Tools: Cable Machines, Jetters and Cameras

Main line cleaning begins at the cleanout. A large cable machine with a cutting head drills through the blockage and shreds roots, paper and debris. If the cable keeps hitting resistance, a jetter scours the pipe wall, and a camera confirms the line is clear and finds anything the cable cannot fix.

Jetting is covered in depth on our hydro jetting page. This page is about the full job, from the first emergency clear to the follow-up that prevents the next one.

Why Main Lines Clog in Magna Neighborhoods

Roots

Mature trees near older lots reach joints in clay and cast iron.

Grease and wipes

Kitchen fat and flushed wipes pack the line.

Scale and film

Mineral-rich water leaves coating inside pipes.

Belly or offset

A low spot or shifted joint catches solids.

Magna Water District's 2023 report shows total dissolved solids averaging 639 mg/L, well above the 500 mg/L guideline utilities use for taste and appearance. Those minerals add to scale and film in drain lines. The effect is slow, which is why a main line often clogs after years rather than months.

Septic and Private Systems at the Edge of the Valley

Most homes in Magna connect to municipal sewer, but outer properties toward Stansbury Park and Tooele may use septic systems. A septic backup is a different problem and needs a pumper and a tank inspection. Tell dispatch what kind of system you have so the right truck is sent.
