What Magna Express Plumbing Does

Magna Express Plumbing is a call-in plumbing service for Magna, Utah and the towns around it. You call (385) 488-1892, a dispatcher listens to the problem, and a licensed and insured plumber is scheduled to come to your home or business.

The line is open 24 hours a day because plumbing failures do not keep office hours. A split supply line at midnight and a clogged drain on a Sunday morning get the same attention as a weekday call.

Our office address is 8980 W Magna Main St, Magna, UT 84044. Jobs are carried out by licensed plumbers dispatched from that hub across Magna, West Valley City, Kearns, Taylorsville and West Jordan. We do not publish individual plumber names or license numbers on this website, because the plumber who arrives depends on the job and the hour.

How a Call Works

Describe it

Dispatch asks what you see, hear and smell, and where the water shutoff is.

Get scheduled

Emergencies are sent first, and other work gets a same-day appointment when available.

Hear the price

The plumber diagnoses the problem and quotes the repair before starting.

Approve and fix

You approve the work, the repair is done, and the system is tested.

That order matters. Many homeowners have been burned by a plumber who starts work first and explains the bill afterward. Our process puts the price in front of you while the decision is still yours, and you are free to say no and call someone else.

Checking Any Plumber's License in Utah

Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing, which is part of the state Department of Commerce. The license classes are apprentice, journeyman and master, plus residential journeyman and residential master. To become licensed a plumber passes a written theory exam and a practical exam, after logging required work and school hours.

Anyone can verify a plumber in a minute. Ask for the plumber's full name, open the state's public license lookup at secure.utah.gov, and search by name. The division's plumbing desk can answer questions at (801) 530-6628 on weekdays.

Questions worth asking any plumber

  • What is your full name and license class, and may I look you up on the state lookup?
  • Will you give me the price in writing or by text before work starts?
  • Is the work covered by a warranty on parts and labor, and what does it exclude?
  • Does the repair need a permit, and who pulls it? Ask your city building department which jobs it requires.
  • What happens if the job turns out to be bigger once the wall is open?
