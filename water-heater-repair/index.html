No hot water is an emergency when the tank is leaking, when a gas smell is present, or when the home has babies, older residents or a cold night ahead. Our 24/7 line takes water heater calls at any hour. If you see water around the tank, shut off the cold supply valve on top and switch off the heater before you phone.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. Do not relight a pilot in a room that smells like gas.

When No Hot Water Cannot Wait

Plan on same-day service if water is pooling under the heater, the relief valve is discharging, or the cabinet is hot and rumbling. A tank that leaks from the bottom is rarely repairable, and the sooner it is drained, the less floor you lose. A tank with no heat but no leak can usually wait for a morning appointment.

As a rough guide, a tank that is under about ten years old with a failed part is worth repairing. A tank past that age with a leak, or one that needs a second repair in a short time, usually costs more to keep running than to replace. Hard Water and Magna Water Heaters

Magna Water District's 2023 report put hardness at an average of 105 mg/L, which is moderately hard, and total dissolved solids at an average of 639 mg/L. In a tank heater, that mineral content settles as scale on the bottom and coats heating elements.

Scaled elements burn out sooner, and a layer of sediment makes a gas burner run longer to heat the same water. Flushing the tank once a year and replacing the anode rod when it is spent are the two cheapest ways to add years to a heater in this water.

What the Plumber Checks on a Service Call

A typical diagnostic visit

  • Tests water temperature at the heater and at a faucet to find where heat is lost.
  • Inspects the pilot, thermocouple and burner on gas units, or elements and thermostats on electric units.
  • Checks the temperature and pressure relief valve and the discharge pipe.
  • Looks for sediment, rust at fittings and a worn anode rod.
  • Verifies venting and gas connections on gas heaters, and the expansion tank where one is required.

Hybrid and heat pump water heaters have their own parts and need a plumber familiar with them, so mention the model on the call if you know it. Gas units with scorched venting or back-drafting need immediate attention, since carbon monoxide is the risk.

Safety Standards and Repairs

Repairs that touch gas valves, burners, venting or relief valves are safety work. Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with statewide amendments, and your city or county building department enforces it. A relief valve that drips, a flue that spills exhaust or a heater without a proper discharge line is a code problem as well as a repair, and a plumber should correct it before the unit goes back into service.
