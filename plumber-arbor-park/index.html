Arbor Park is a newer part of west Magna, centered near West 3500 South and 8400 West. Townhomes, apartments and recently built houses sit close to the Arbor Park Shopping Center, and the plumbing questions here differ from those on Magna Main Street.

Where Arbor Park Is

The Arbor Park community listed by its builder, Holmes Homes, sits at 8269 West Cascade View Way. The Arbor Park Shopping Center is at 8382 West Arbor Park Drive, and an apartment community shares the Arbor Park Drive name. Bus stops at 3500 South and 8400 West sit a short walk away.

We found no official neighborhood boundary, so we describe the area by those streets. A nearby community, Mahogany Ridge by Ivory Homes, sits at 8216 West Golden Trout Way along the same stretch.

A real estate guide places this area among the newest housing in Magna, with a mix of townhouses and detached homes. Build years vary by community, and we do not guess at them. Marketing for the area mentions views of the Oquirrh Mountains and access to downtown Salt Lake City by State Route 201.

Newer Homes Have Their Own Plumbing Questions

Newer construction does not mean trouble free, and it does not mean problem prone. It means different questions. Is the builder warranty still active? Who maintains a shared line in a townhome group? Where does the water heater sit, and can you reach the shutoff without a ladder?

Check your warranty paperwork and homeowners association documents before you hire anyone for work that might be covered. If a repair is not covered, we will explain what we found and the price before we start.

Water Quality and Your Fixtures

Magna Water District reported moderately hard water in its 2023 report, with hardness averaging 105 mg/L. Minerals in hard water leave scale on heating elements and faucet aerators in any home, new or old. Flushing a tank heater once a year slows that buildup.

Your bill shows which provider serves your address. If you are considering treatment, see our water softener page for how sizing and installation work.

Simple habits that help in any townhome or new home

  • Learn where the main shutoff is and turn it once a year so it does not seize.
  • Know which fixture valves serve the kitchen, baths and laundry.
  • Flush the water heater once a year according to the maker's guide.
  • Keep a bucket and a towel near the water heater.
  • Read your warranty and association papers before you call for covered work.
