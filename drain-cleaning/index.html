A drain that will not clear gets urgent fast, especially when a tub or floor drain starts filling with water that is not yours. If you are reading this with a sink full of standing water, stop using the fixtures and call the 24/7 line. We handle drain emergencies at night and on weekends, and a plumber can often be scheduled the same day.

While you wait, do not pour chemical cleaner into a full drain. It sits in the water, burns skin and makes the plumber's job less safe. Bail what you can into a bucket and keep laundry and dishwashers off.

When a Clogged Drain Becomes an Emergency

When to call the emergency line

  • Water or sewage rising in a tub, shower or floor drain when you flush or run the washer.
  • Every drain in the house is slow at once, which points to the main line.
  • A toilet gurgles when another fixture drains.
  • A kitchen sink overflows and food waste or grease is coming back up.
  • A basement floor drain shows water or a sewage smell.

A single slow sink is different. That is a same-day or next-day appointment, not a midnight call, and we will tell you so honestly when you phone.

Why Drains Clog in Magna

Magna Water District reported total dissolved solids averaging 639 mg/L in 2023, with hardness near 105 mg/L. Those minerals do not clog a drain on their own, but they bond with soap and grease to form a hard film inside pipes, which narrows the passage over the years.

That narrowed pipe catches hair, wipes and kitchen fat much sooner. In older houses near Main Street the drain lines can be rougher inside, which gives roots and debris more to grab. Newer homes farther out clog mostly from grease, flushed wipes and builder-grade traps.

Kitchen Sink Clogs: The Most Common Call

Kitchen drains clog more often than any other fixture in the house. Cooking fat cools inside the pipe and coats the walls, and every rinse adds another layer. Coffee grounds and rice swell and pack the trap. A garbage disposal helps with food scraps but does nothing for grease.

For a kitchen clog the plumber usually removes the trap, runs a small cable through the branch, and rinses the line with hot water. If the sink is connected to a disposal, the disposal outlet is checked as well. See garbage disposal repair if the unit itself is jammed or leaking.

Snaking, Cabling and When to Step Up to Jetting

A drain snake, or cable machine, drills through a blockage and pulls it apart. It is the right tool for hair, paper and soft clogs. When a cable keeps punching a hole that closes again in a few weeks, the pipe walls are probably coated and the answer is scrubbing the whole pipe with high-pressure water. That is the work described on our hydro jetting page.

If a cable stops at the same spot every time, a camera inspection will show whether the cause is a root, a belly in the pipe or a crushed section. We explain the camera process on the sewer inspection page.

Drain Line Leaks and Slow Problems

Sometimes a drain does not clog but leaks, with a damp cabinet floor or a stain on the ceiling below a bathroom. A leaking trap or a cracked drain line needs a repair, not a cable. Tell dispatch what you see and the plumber will bring parts for either result.

Standards That Matter for Drains

Clearing a drain does not usually need a permit, but replacing sections of pipe can. Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with statewide amendments, and your city or county building department enforces it. That code governs trap, vent and slope rules, which is why a drain that is slow because of poor slope may need a plumber to correct the layout instead of simply cleaning it.
