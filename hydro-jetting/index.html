Hydro jetting is the answer when a drain clogs, gets snaked, and clogs again a few weeks later. A jetter pumps water through a hose and nozzle at high pressure, scouring grease, scale and root hairs off the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the middle. We treat a backup that keeps returning as urgent, and the 24/7 line can dispatch a jetting crew, depending on the day, for after-hours sewer backups.

Backups That Cannot Wait

A sewer backup is an emergency when water rises in a tub or floor drain, or sewage reaches finished floors. Stop all water use, keep people and pets out of the affected room, and call. Jetting is only part of an emergency response, since the plumber first needs to know what is blocking the line.

A cable cuts a hole through a clog. A jetter cleans the full circumference, which is why it lasts longer. The basic drain cleaning page explains when a cable is enough, and the two tools often work together on the same visit.

What Hydro Jetting Removes in Magna Lines

Mineral film is a local factor. Magna Water District reported total dissolved solids averaging 639 mg/L in 2023, and the minerals bind with soap and fat in drain lines to form a hard coating. Over years that coating shrinks the pipe diameter and traps debris.

Roots are the other common cause on older lots with mature trees. Fine root hairs enter at joints and grow into the flow. Jetting cuts and flushes them, but it does not repair the joint, so roots often return until the pipe is lined or replaced.

When Jetting Is Not the Right Tool

A crushed pipe, a major belly that holds water, or a heavily corroded cast iron line can be damaged by high-pressure water. In those cases the camera shows the real problem and the fix is repair or lining, as described on the sewer line repair page. A responsible plumber will not jet a pipe that cannot take it.

Good candidates for jetting

  • A line that clogs again within weeks of snaking.
  • Restaurants and shops with heavy grease loads.
  • Older cast iron or clay lines with rough interior scale.
  • Main lines with root hairs but no collapse.
  • Preventive cleaning before a camera inspection or a sale.

Equipment Standards and Safe Practice

Jetting equipment runs at pressures that can injure a person or damage a weak pipe, so operators are trained and the nozzle stays inside the pipe. Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with statewide amendments, and your city or county building department enforces it. That code governs drain layout, so when the camera shows a layout problem, the fix is a plumbing repair and not another cleaning.
