A sewer camera inspection is the fastest way to stop guessing about a drain problem. A waterproof camera on a flexible cable travels through the pipe and sends live video to a screen, so the plumber and you can see roots, cracks, grease and sags. It is also the standard check before buying a house, lining a pipe or finishing a basement.

Pre-Purchase Inspections for Magna Homes

Buyers in older parts of Magna should consider a camera run before closing. A house near Main Street may carry decades-old clay or cast iron, while a home built more recently probably has PVC. The camera tells you which, and whether the lateral has been repaired before.

A sewer repair can cost more than most fix-up items on an inspection report, so seeing the line first gives you leverage in negotiation. Ask the plumber for the recording and a written summary you can hand to the seller.

How an Inspection Visit Goes

Find the access

The plumber locates a cleanout or pulls a toilet to enter the line.

Run the camera

The camera is fed down the pipe while distance is recorded.

Mark the problems

A locator pinpoints the depth and surface position of any defect.

Review the footage

You see the video and hear what each finding means.

If the camera finds a blockage that stops it, the line is cleared first through sewer line cleaning, then the camera continues. A line that needs repair moves to the plan described on the sewer line repair page.

Cameras for Drain Lines and Storm Drains

The same method works on branch drain lines, floor drains and storm drains. Property owners with catch basins or yard drains can use a camera to confirm they connect where they should. Mention the drain type when you call so the plumber brings the right head size.

Why Pipes Deserve a Look After Strong Shaking

Magna was the epicenter area of the March 18, 2020 magnitude 5.7 earthquake, according to Utah's earthquake information site. Ground that moves enough to open small cracks can also shift underground lines. If you have never had your lateral inspected, a camera is the cleanest way to know whether it came through.

Ask for these in the inspection report

  • Distance to every defect from the cleanout.
  • Pipe material and diameter along the run.
  • Surface marks and depth for any repair point.
  • A digital copy of the video.
  • A plain statement of whether action is needed now or can wait.
